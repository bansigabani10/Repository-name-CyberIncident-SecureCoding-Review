SOCIAL ENGINEERING

Six Manipulation Techniques

😨

Fear

Attackers create fear by threatening consequences such as account closure, security incidents or financial loss.

⏰

Urgency

Victims are pressured to act quickly before they have time to verify the request.

👔

Authority

Attackers pretend to be managers, IT staff, banks or other trusted authorities.

🔍

Curiosity

Interesting or unexpected information is used to make people open links, files or messages.

🎁

Reward

Attackers offer prizes, discounts, bonuses or other rewards to encourage unsafe actions.

🤝

Trust

Attackers build familiarity or pretend to be someone the victim already knows.
