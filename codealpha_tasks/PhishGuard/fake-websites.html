WEBSITE SAFETY

Before You Log In

Use these checks whenever you visit a website that requests sensitive information.

🌐

Check the Domain

Carefully inspect the complete domain name. Don't rely only on the appearance of the page.

🔐

Don't Trust HTTPS Alone

HTTPS encrypts the connection, but a fraudulent website can also use HTTPS.

🔑

Protect Credentials

Don't enter passwords or banking details into a website unless you have verified its legitimacy.

🛑

Stop When Unsure

If something feels unusual, stop and access the organization's official website through a trusted route.
