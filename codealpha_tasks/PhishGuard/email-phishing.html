LEARNING CHECKLIST

Common Phishing Warning Signs

📧

Suspicious Sender

Check the sender's email address carefully for unusual domains, spelling mistakes or impersonation.

🚨

Urgent Language

Attackers often create fear and urgency to make victims act quickly.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Don't click unexpected links. Verify the destination before entering credentials.

🔐

Information Requests

Be cautious when an unexpected message asks for passwords, financial information or other sensitive data.
